Frequently asked questions

I did not receive a verification email.
Check Spam, Junk and All Mail, then search for “Firebase” or “Verify your email”. Make sure you entered the correct address.
I cannot sign in.
Check your email and password, or use “Send password reset email” on the Register page.
Can I deposit or withdraw real crypto?
No. This website is currently a prototype. Real deposits, withdrawals, custody and live trading are not enabled. Do not send funds to this demo.
What information should I send?
Describe the issue and include the page or error message. Never send passwords, SMS codes, private keys, seed phrases or identity documents by email.
