Privacy Overview

A plain-language overview of information that may be processed when you use OnChainSpro.

Information and account features

OnChainSpro may process account and profile details, authentication information, support messages, security-related events and activity associated with the features you use. Keep passwords, one-time verification codes, private keys and wallet recovery phrases secure and share them with no one, including through support messages.

Service providers

OnChainSpro uses Firebase services for website hosting and, where enabled, Firebase Authentication and Firestore for account and application features. Market information is obtained through external data providers, whose privacy notices and service terms explain how they handle requests.

Why information is used

Information is used, as applicable, to operate account features, maintain platform security, respond to support requests, troubleshoot technical issues and improve the service. The information involved corresponds to the features enabled and used on the platform.

Your choices and questions

Manage your account through the controls available on the website. For questions about personal information or to make a privacy request, contact support@onchainspro.com. For your security, include only the details needed to explain your request and keep passwords, verification codes and wallet credentials private.

This overview is intended to explain OnChainSpro's privacy approach alongside the platform's technical configuration and applicable legal requirements. Information may be stored, retained or processed by OnChainSpro and its service providers as needed to deliver, secure and support the services described above.

Last reviewed: 7 November 2026. This overview reflects OnChainSpro's account, website and support features and is updated as the platform's services and privacy practices evolve.
