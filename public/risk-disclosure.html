Market Information & Risk Guide

Explore cryptocurrency and gold markets with clear information about our market data, charts and platform features.

About this information: OnChainSpro brings market information, interactive charts and account features together in one place. Content on this page is provided for general information and educational purposes; it is not personalised financial, investment, tax or legal advice.

Explore cryptocurrency and gold markets

Follow market movements and discover supported digital assets and precious metals through the OnChainSpro platform. Cryptocurrency and precious-metal markets operate continuously or according to their respective market schedules, and prices can move as market conditions change. Liquidity, spreads, volatility and other market factors may affect prices and transaction outcomes. Take time to understand the features you use and make decisions that reflect your own objectives and circumstances.

Market data and interactive charts

Market pages display information supplied by external data providers to help you follow prices and review market activity. Data availability and update frequency depend on the relevant provider and asset. For time-sensitive decisions, check the latest available information and compare it with other reliable sources.

Platform tools and account features

OnChainSpro provides account features and interactive workflows designed to make it easier to explore supported markets and understand how platform tools operate. Feature descriptions and on-screen labels explain the function of each workflow. Where a balance or result is specifically labelled as practice or simulated, it is for platform exploration only and does not represent a deposit, customer-held cryptocurrency or a completed exchange transaction.

Making informed decisions

Use market information as one part of your own research. Before using a feature, review its description and the details shown during the workflow. Cryptocurrency and precious-metal markets involve risk, and values can rise or fall. No particular return or outcome is guaranteed. If you need advice about your personal circumstances, consider speaking with an appropriately qualified independent professional.
