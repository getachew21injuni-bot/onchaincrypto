🌐 General

What is OnChainCrypto?⌄
OnChainCrypto is a cryptocurrency market information and trading-interface project designed to help users explore digital-asset markets. The current website is a prototype; institutional-grade execution, metals and stock trading, and custody services are not currently enabled.
Is OnChainCrypto safe to use?⌄
Use a unique password and protect your email account. The website uses Firebase sign-in, but cold storage, bank-grade custody and two-factor authentication are not confirmed as operational here. Do not deposit funds or share passwords, codes, private keys or recovery phrases.
How do I get started?⌄
Create an account with your name and email, follow the sign-in or verification steps shown, then explore Markets and the paper-trading interface. Real deposits and live trading are not enabled, so do not send funds.

📈 Trading

Which trading pairs are available?⌄
The market page displays selected crypto assets such as BTC, ETH, BNB and SOL using public market data. The listed pairs, metals (XAU/XAG) and stocks (AAPL, TSLA, GOOG, MSFT) are not all connected as live tradable markets on this prototype.
How do I place a trade?⌄
Open Trade, choose an available asset, review the chart and order details, select Buy or Sell, and review the duration if shown. Current trades are simulated paper trades, not real exchange orders.
What are the trading fees?⌄
A verified live fee schedule has not been configured for this prototype. Maker fees of 0.10% and taker fees of 0.15% should not be treated as active fees unless they are formally confirmed and published by the platform operator.

🔐 Account & Security

How do I enable two-factor authentication (2FA)?⌄
Authenticator-app 2FA is not currently confirmed as implemented in this website. Check the account security settings for available options; do not assume 2FA is active until setup is explicitly provided and completed.
What if I forget my password?⌄
Open the Register/Login page and choose the password-reset option. Follow the email instructions. Check your spam folder if the message does not arrive.
How do I verify my account?⌄
Email verification depends on your sign-in flow. Full identity-document KYC processing is not configured yet, so do not email identity documents to support.

💱 Deposits & Withdrawals

How long do deposits take?⌄
No live deposit processing is enabled on this prototype, so processing times cannot be promised. On a functioning crypto service, timing depends on network confirmations and the provider's review process.
What is the minimum withdrawal?⌄
Minimum withdrawal limits have not been configured for live withdrawals. Do not rely on any amount shown until official coin-specific limits and fees are published.
How can I deposit or withdraw funds?⌄
The Deposit and Withdraw pages are interface previews; real transfers are not enabled. Do not send crypto to an address shown or supplied outside a verified, operational deposit flow.

🧰 Technical Support

I cannot register or sign in. What should I do?⌄
Check your internet connection, confirm your email and password, and try the password-reset option. If the problem continues, contact support with the error message—never include your password or verification code.
Market prices or charts are not loading. Why?⌄
Market data and charts rely on external providers and your connection. Refresh the page, check again later, and contact support if the issue persists.
How do I contact technical support?⌄
Use New ticket or Contact in this Support Center to prepare an email to onchaincryptosupport@gmail.com. The mailbox must be created and monitored by the site operator to receive and answer messages.
