⇵FinancialWallet education, financial knowledge and responsible trading⌄
BUILD YOUR FINANCIAL KNOWLEDGE

Your financial journey starts with knowledge.

Learn how digital assets, market movements and risk management work. Set clear goals, make thoughtful decisions and give your strategy time to develop.

◈ Understanding your wallet⌄

A crypto wallet is a tool for viewing and managing digital assets. Learn about balances, supported networks, wallet addresses and private-key security before using any financial service.

⇩ Deposits — what to know⌄

A deposit means transferring digital assets into a wallet or platform. Always check the asset, network and destination address carefully. Sending assets to the wrong network or address may result in permanent loss.

⇧ Withdrawals — what to know⌄

A withdrawal sends assets from a platform to an external wallet. Before any real withdrawal, review network fees, processing times, destination details and applicable platform rules.

◷ Financial activity and records⌄

Keep clear records of trades, fees, deposits and withdrawals. Review transaction dates, amounts and status regularly so you can understand your financial activity and spot anything unexpected.

✦ Grow through learning and discipline⌄

Successful investing is not about trading as often as possible. Focus on understanding the market, setting realistic goals, managing risk and avoiding decisions driven by fear or excitement. More trading does not guarantee more profit.

  • Learn before you invest and only risk money you can afford to lose.
  • Use a plan, set limits and review your decisions regularly.
  • Diversify thoughtfully instead of relying on one asset.
  • Be patient, stay consistent and treat losses as a reason to reassess.
  • Be cautious of anyone promising guaranteed returns or fast profits.

🌐 General

What is Onchain?⌄
Onchain brings crypto and Gold market information, interactive charts, asset conversion tools and practice together. Explore supported markets, compare prices and review your portfolio activity in one place. practice trades and conversions are simulations; they do not place live exchange orders or move blockchain assets.
Is Onchain safe to use?⌄
Use a unique password and review the security options available in your account. Keep passwords, verification codes, private keys and recovery phrases private. Additional security services will be described here as they become available.
How do I get started?⌄
Create your account, explore Markets and charts, compare supported assets with Convert, and use practice to become familiar with the platform.

📈 Trading

Which trading pairs are available?⌄
Markets presents selected public crypto-market information. Asset coverage and price availability depend on the connected data providers.
How do I place a trade?⌄
Open Trade, choose a supported asset, review the market information, and select Buy or Sell. Orders in this workspace are practice trades, not live exchange orders.
What are the trading fees?⌄
practice trades do not execute on an exchange, so no live exchange trading fee is charged. Any fees and limits for live services will be displayed before those services are offered.

🔐 Account & Security

How do I enable two-factor authentication (2FA)?⌄
Visit Settings to review the security options currently available for your account. Further verification options will be announced when ready.
What if I forget my password?⌄
Choose password reset on the Register/Login page. Check spam if no email arrives.
How do I verify my account?⌄
Open Identity Verification to review the requested details and the current review status. Document images are preview-only in this version; use the in-platform form for the available text details.

💱 Deposits & Withdrawals

How long do deposits take?⌄
The wallet currently provides deposit guidance; live blockchain deposit processing is not connected. Do not send cryptocurrency until an operational deposit service and verified address are explicitly provided.
What is the minimum withdrawal?⌄
Live withdrawal minimums and network fees are not active. The wallet currently provides account and asset information; it does not send payouts.
How can I deposit or withdraw funds?⌄
Open Wallet and choose Deposit or Withdraw to review the guided screens. These screens do not submit blockchain transfers or move real funds. Only use a deposit service after its live status and destination address have been verified.

🧰 Technical Support

I cannot register or sign in. What should I do?⌄
Check your internet connection and sign-in details, then use password reset if needed. If you contact support, share the error message and page name, while keeping passwords and verification codes private.
Market prices or charts are not loading. Why?⌄
Refresh the Markets page and check your connection. Price updates depend on public data providers, so a short delay may occur. Contact support if the chart remains unavailable.
How do I contact technical support?⌄
Sign in and open Help and Guidance to send a private support request. Replies appear in your Onchain account.
